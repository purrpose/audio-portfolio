ABOUT

About Me

I'm a composer and sound designer focused on games and interactive media.

I've been producing music for 7+ years, working across electronic, cinematic and guitar-driven styles. I've contributed music and sound to multiple game jam projects and currently work on an upcoming visual novel.

My background in IT also gives me a strong interest in the technical side of game audio and interactive sound.
